The public card describes Phoveus' market-clock and read-only DeFi analytics capabilities. The PancakeSwap and Venus endpoints must be reachable on production before registering this card.

## What ERC-8004 registration does

ERC-8004 mints an ERC-721 identity NFT. The registry's register(string agentURI) call points that identity at the public registration JSON and returns an agentId. The card alone is not proof of registration.

## Mainnet registration checklist

1. Confirm the production card URL above returns valid JSON and describes the deployed capabilities accurately.
2. Confirm the campaign wallet is the wallet intended to own the agent NFT, and that it is connected to **BNB Smart Chain mainnet (chain ID 56)**.
3. Open the Identity Registry on BscScan: https://bscscan.com/address/0x8004A169FB4a3325136EB29fA0ceB6D2e539a432#writeContract
4. Connect the campaign wallet on BscScan, select register(string agentURI), and enter exactly:
   https://phoveus.vercel.app/.well-known/agent-registration.json
5. Review the network, registry address, gas fee, and wallet account before signing. Do not share a private key, seed phrase, or wallet password.
6. After confirmation, record the transaction hash and emitted Registered(agentId, agentURI, owner) event.
7. Independently verify on BNB Chain that ownerOf(agentId) matches the campaign wallet and tokenURI(agentId) matches the public card URL.
8. Only after that verification, update the card's registrations array to include:
   ~~~json
   {
     "agentId": "<verified numeric agentId>",
     "agentRegistry": "eip155:56:0x8004A169FB4a3325136EB29fA0ceB6D2e539a432"
   }
   ~~~
   Replace the placeholder with the actual numeric ID. Do not invent an ID or mark registration complete before verification.

## Safety and claims

- Registration requires a real transaction signed by the campaign wallet; this repository change cannot sign on the user's behalf.
- Keep private keys and seed phrases out of Git, chat, .env files committed to the repository, and deployment logs.
- ERC-8004 identity registration does not itself prove that a service is secure, profitable, audited, or capable of executing transactions.
- PancakeSwap pool analytics and Venus market/APY data are read-only and informational. No swap, deposit, or redemption is enabled by this integration.
